Starry Night

The Starry Night is an 1889 oil-on-canvas painting by Dutch Post-Impressionist artist Vincent van Gogh. It is housed in the permanent collection of the Museum of Modern Art (MoMA) in New York City and is widely considered a foundational masterpiece of modern art

  • Artist: Vincent van Gogh (1853–1890)
  • Year: 1889
  • Medium: Oil on canvas
  • Dimensions: 73.7 × 92.1 cm
